Reiatsu repair: `handlers.js` delegates the native `0x01811400` request to `reiatsu.js`. Both the window's i16-count/i8-kind packet and the processor's u32-count/u32-kind packet are supported. Existing training costs and 50 EXP per turn are preserved. The level/EXP state is cloned, saved before emitting updates, and failed saves refund the resource cost.

Every owned hero receives a complete `0x01180104` stat update before the training response. Equipment/stat construction converts Reiatsu Power/Intelligence/Agile/Life bonuses into their physical attack/defense, magic attack/defense, speed and HP contributions, calibrated to that hero's base attribute block. Login, base/PvP stat groups and combat use the same construction. These contributions are included before reincarnation, profession refinement and hero-star multipliers.

Stats increase on a Reiatsu level-up; training below the EXP threshold only advances EXP. `node sunucu/tests/reiatsu.test.js` verifies both packet layouts, exact costs, real primary and combat gains, owned-hero refresh, persistence and save-failure refunds. Reincarnation, Academy and boss/mail regression tests also pass. Test data is isolated from the player's files.
