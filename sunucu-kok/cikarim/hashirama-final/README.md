## Combat

HP >=60% selects Strong Sword - Crusher and Severe Thousand Hands; lower HP selects Taijutsu Expert and Adamantine Fist. Selection happens at action time, including after healing. Damaging moves scale with Physical ATK while physical/mixed types determine mitigation.

Deity Gates: 20 Chakra, 3-round recovery, 7 targets, Interruption and selected-target Deity Gate Seal II. At >=60% HP additionally execute Golem once that round. Seal prevents Mysteries, forces Critical Hits and locks Critical damage to 1.5x.

Rumbling Dragon in Burst: 40 Chakra, 2-round recovery, unavoidable damage to 12 targets, immunity suppression through round end, absorb the enemy shared Chakra pool, selected-target Low Float and Weakness II through next round end. Advanced Immunity resists lower-level suppression. Mysteries share recovery, with one cast per form per round (at most two total). Damage/Chase reductions can make Burst ready after Deity Gates, but cannot repeat the same form within that round.

Reactive AI: Hashirama, Pain and Obito recheck their current Mystery after complete action/Chase/revival chains, including ordinary Bleach combos. Prompt Mysteries do not require an unused standard action. TP, Chakra, cooldowns, control effects and per-round cast budgets still apply. Pain can spend points gained after his standard or later hits, including his second eligible Final cast. Obito keeps shared form recovery and persistent Rage. The scheduler waits for all round-start effects and opening Mysteries before reactive selection. Verify with `sunucu/tests/ninja-reactive-mystery.test.js` (both camps and real encoded AI reports).

Cooldown update: all three ninjas now have zero cooldown on every non-Mystery move. Standards and Chases retain their action/trigger budgets. Shurado Laser and automatic Rage Kamui execute once at every eligible round start; these round markers reset for a fresh battle. All seven Mystery cooldowns remain unchanged. `sunucu/tests/ninja-no-cooldowns.test.js` covers consecutive rounds and both camps. `cikarim/ninja-cooldowns.py` updates the existing native skill descriptions without rebuilding animation textures.

Taijutsu/Adamantine: 35% life steal. Taijutsu: +20% crit, 40% High Float chance. Sword: random occupied line, Knockdown, Standard attempts -1 per hit unit, +30% damage to Super Armor, 35% extra-Standard chance, hard cap 5 attempts. A lost Standard subtracts one attempt from multi-attack heroes too.

Chases share two charges per round across all variants. Severe: 10 Combo, Knockdown, Interruption, Mystery recovery -1. Base Severe inflicts irremovable Enfeeble ignoring immunity; Burst Severe inflicts Weakness II. Both suppress Attack/Ninjutsu to 75% base. Burst Chases ignore 30% defenses; Severe against prior Weakness caps evasion at 20% until defeat/battle end. Golem: 12 primary victims and 25% orthogonal splash per affected neighbor.

Each positive damage event grants 0.5 Technique Point (max 2; Defense +20% per point). At 2 consume all, recover up to 40 Chakra and enter Burst. First damage per round grants Excited (+60% Attack/Ninjutsu, -30% Defense/Resistance). Base Senjutsu: debuff immunity, heal 25% max HP, Mystery recovery -1 per damage event. Burst replaces healing/reduction with Advanced/Fire immunity. A living Burst Hashirama grants the damaging ally +3% Attack/Ninjutsu stacks for the respective damage type. Entering/leaving Burst grants stacking +10% Mystery damage; point cycles within an existing Burst do not count as another activation.

Burst ends at round end, restores base skills and triggers Deep Forest/Explosive for 2 rounds, also active at battle start. Mysteries from Explosive units under Deep Forest inflict Root Binding II on hit enemies: no Standards, Defense/Resistance fixed to base while the forest remains active. The caster is never rooted.

Unspecified damage coefficients, chances, penetration and Burst damage bonus are explicit balance choices in `sunucu/hashirama-final.json`. Specified Mystery cooldowns remain 3/2, Chase twice per round, Golem once per round.
