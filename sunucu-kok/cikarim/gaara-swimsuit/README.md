# Gaara [Swimsuit]

Hero `11120991`, SSS / quality 7, all fifteen formation positions in either camp. Modify Fragment `14130991`, inventory icon `16228143`, owned-only Mystic fragment offer `41120991`. The existing reserved-ID filter prevents selling this hero in the admin overlay.

All damaging moves use buffed Physical ATK against Physical DEF. Mystery hits up to seven enemies with Knockdown on the selected enemy, cooldown 3, battlefield cooldown 1 and Chakra 20. A selected enemy with Suppress Super Armor grants bonus critical chance and Level 2 Super Armor to Gaara until round end. Regular Super Armor blocks displacement; its level 2 variant also ignores lower-level suppression.

Sand Guard shields two or three living allies for 20% of Gaara's initial Resistance, independently of buffs, and removes exactly one removable debuff from each selected ally. This support formula deliberately remains based on Resistance. It causes no attack damage.

Every living Gaara executes unavoidable Sandstorm against four front enemies at the start of each round, followed once by unavoidable Sand Surfing against up to six enemies. Discouragement II blocks receiving new external positive buffs/stat increases/shields until the end of the next round or Mystery execution; pre-existing buffs and self-buffs remain. Sand Surfing suppresses regular Super Armor until round end and stacks a 15-percentage-point critical-rate reduction. Critical hits inflict Blindness II, blocking standards for the current round. The ordinary single-debuff cleanse cannot remove level 2 effects.

While any Gaara is alive, a non-Summer/non-Swimsuit unit's first damage of the round triggers 50% standard-attack damage reduction and 40% healing-received reduction for that round. Both teams are affected. Shield absorption, shared hits, reflected damage and ordinary damage-over-time ticks are included. Already applied reductions retain their stated round duration after Gaara dies. Summer/Swimsuit flags exempt matching units; Gaara is inherently exempt.

The screenshot does not specify damage coefficients, proc probability for two versus three shields, or the bonus critical percentage. These are explicit tuning values: Mystery 1.8, Sandstorm 0.3, Sand Surfing 0.8, 50/50 shield count, bonus critical rate 30 percentage points. Edit `sunucu/gaara-swimsuit.json` for tuning; no attack formula uses Magic ATK or Resistance.

The supplied `Gaara_Swimsuit_Full` actor/effect timelines are decoded losslessly with original pivots. Standard uses A_3hit, Mystery and additional surfing use SP_skill1, Sandstorm uses SP_skill3. Skill sequences and caches are exclusive to Gaara. Source wave animation and native hit timing are aligned; per-frame motion approaches the target smoothly and returns after the effect. Large wave edges are feathered to avoid visible rectangular backplates. The native Mecha-style player reuses bitmap surfaces and paged <=2048px ARGB streams.
