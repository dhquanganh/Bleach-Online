# Battle playback x1 / x2

Client **2026101962** adds a red/gold speed toggle immediately to the right of the native Skip battle button. It displays the current multiplier and highlights x2. It is available independently of Skip/VIP permissions, follows the Skip bounds when resizing and hides with the battle controls. Each battle begins at x1.

The original Flash stage FPS is captured at battle entrance and doubled at x2. Character, pet, native effect and MovieClip timelines therefore advance through their existing frames, including every damage dispatch marker. Battle-only elapsed-time effects use a continuous scaled clock, with no jump when toggling. Command pauses and skill banners use cancelable virtual-time deadlines, so pending waits respond to both x1→x2 and x2→x1 changes. Completed callbacks are removed before invocation and all pending callbacks are cleared at battle end. Battle tween durations are divided by the selected multiplier when created; an already running tween finishes its current segment at its original duration. Custom status notices retain their existing reading time.

Natural end, Skip, Reset, hiding the battle and removal from the stage restore the original FPS and clear the battle scheduler. Server clocks, rewards, turn cooldowns, damage coefficients and encoded combat reports are unchanged.

`build.py --ffdec JAR --install` imports nine specific classes from the live 1961 source export and installs the protected canonical/versioned client plus its version manifest. It checks that the source export still matches the live client before compiling or writing and preserves non-code SWF tags. The original protected client and version manifest are backed up locally. The builder refuses to overwrite a later client with stale input.

Validation: `build-probe.py --ffdec JAR`, then launch `runtime-probe.js` in the bundled Electron/Flash 22 runtime without ELECTRON_RUN_AS_NODE, and run `verify.py`. The isolated preview uses the same production speed methods and exercises the button, 30/60 FPS, normal/doubled deadlines, both toggles while a callback is pending, cancellation, end cleanup, a new battle and resized Skip positioning. The measured 500ms virtual delay completed in 271ms at x2; actual frame throughput depends on machine load. Sixteen native checks passed. This component preview does not access an account or replace a full interactive battle.
