# Ultimate BP rankings and admin awards

The auth service reads authenticated account/player records from MongoDB and current online-worker files. Scores are real `veri.json.ultimateBP.pieces`; uncreated characters, deleted accounts and scores below the native 5,000-piece minimum are excluded. Equal scores use account ID order for deterministic ranks. The board is pushed to workers every three seconds and written in the exact native RankInfo7 schema (`[{name, serverID, value}]`).

Admin: `/admin` → **Ultimate BP · Xếp hạng & trao quà**. Review current ranks and the six original reward groups, then click **Chốt BXH & trao quà Top 50**. A stale preview is rejected. This freezes a payout snapshot for the active Ultimate BP round (`ultimate-bp-ranking:1`, with an incrementing `round`), including actor, timestamp, ranks, scores and exact item attachments in MongoDB `ultimateBPRankingPayouts`. The live board continues to update; the frozen payout standings remain visible separately. After every reward is persisted, all player records (including below-threshold and outside-Top-50 players) are reset: Crystal Pieces, the gold remainder and round spending become zero; gift claim counters restart. Earned heroes and reward mail remain. Per-account reset receipts and a durable reset-target list make retries safe. The next eligible payout starts a new round and archives the previous snapshot at `ultimate-bp-ranking:1:history:<round>`. A retry of an old award request cannot trigger a new round.

Recipients receive protected mail, including the original title tokens for Top 1–3. Online workers enqueue/persist mail before acknowledging; offline players receive it in their saved player record. Per-account login/admin locks and DB compare-and-set protect offline delivery. A persistent receipt outside the mailbox prevents duplicates even after claimed mail is deleted. Pending Myoboku/Ultimate BP/Arena redo snapshots are applied before offline delivery to avoid a later replay losing the reward. Only acknowledged, DB-synced deliveries are marked complete; retries and server restarts resume the same snapshot. Full bags can receive mail, and claim waits until there is room.
