Modify Fragment `14130997` and Recruit Token `14140997` use the source head icon. The normal inventory token gives the hero; duplicate ownership leaves the token untouched. Water Clone is summon only.

Every damaging move uses `attackStat: "atk"` and `damageType: "physical"`. The physical hit formula reads buffed Physical ATK and Physical DEF. Source descriptions are imported from `asset/Kakuzu_Edo_Tensei_Full`, with the requested screenshot wording for Heart Capture, the three additional skills and Reattachment. The Water Clone starts with 90% of Kakuzu's max HP. While it lives, Kakuzu survives fatal Mystery damage, including legacy instant defeat. A standard attack queues one random Wind, Lightning or Fire additional skill. Wind damages a random enemy row, applies Discouragement and raises allied initiative 3% through the next round. Lightning applies Paralysis through the round and reduces Mystery cooldown by one. Fire suppresses immunity for the round and clears Kakuzu's Ignition. Heart Capture ignores shields and interruption, gains critical chance below 15% target HP, removes Fear, and on a kill heals 20% max HP and increases Physical ATK 40%. Kakuzu is immune to debuffs and has a high chance to evade chase damage. While its Kakuzu owner is alive, the living Water Clone uses Reattachment at round end to revive one random defeated ally at 30% HP; the clone can do this while controlled. It cannot revive Kakuzu or bypass his four-round self-revival delay. Kakuzu does not independently cast the clone's rescue skill. Kakuzu's own fatal damage schedules Reincarnation four rounds after his defeat at 25% HP, cleanses debuffs, resets Mystery cooldown, and summons the clone again. No one time limit is added to this passive.

Native actor frames retain source pivots, aspect ratios and lossless alpha. Profile idle has a stable canvas and is positioned 10 px lower. The Water Clone shares Kakuzu's native model through RoleModel, including the entry and death timelines, eliminating duplicate model downloads. Skill effects use the supplied SWFs and separate actor scoped layers. The skill layers and clone pose listeners clear on reset, interruption, death and stage removal. ## Wiped lineup termination

After the current hit and immediate revival chain resolves, a camp with no living lineup heroes loses immediately. Auxiliary summons, Shadow Clones and a scheduled Kakuzu revival do not keep the match running by themselves. The scheduler blocks further attacks, combo attacks and reactive Mysteries, skips future round/end-round processing for the finished battle, and uses the same lineup predicate for victory results. The four-round Kakuzu delay remains available while another lineup hero survives. `sunucu/tests/battle-defeat.test.js` reproduces the original round-1 wipe that used to emit rounds 2/5/6/9 and repeated resurrections, verifies both camps and surviving auxiliaries, and checks encoded Cross reports stop in the defeat round. These backend changes require restarting the server; the animation client remains 2026102706.
